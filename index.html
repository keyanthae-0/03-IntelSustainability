THROUGH THE AGES

Explore Intel's journey through time, discovering how our
commitment to innovation has shaped a more sustainable future

1968

Intel Founded

Intel is founded by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, beginning a new era of semiconductor innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel introduces the 4004, one of the world's first commercially available microprocessors.

1978

8086 Processor

Intel releases the 8086 processor, establishing technology that would influence generations of personal computers.

2006

Intel Core 2

Intel introduces the Core 2 processor, beginning a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel expands its sustainability commitments through ambitious environmental and social impact goals.

2030

Sustainability Goals

Intel continues working toward renewable energy, reduced emissions, water conservation, and more sustainable manufacturing.
