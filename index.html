1968

Intel Founded

Intel is founded by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, beginning a new era of semiconductor innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel introduces the 4004, one of the world's first commercially available microprocessors.

1978

8086 Processor

Intel releases the 8086 processor, establishing technology that would influence generations of personal computers.

2006

Intel Core 2

Intel introduces the Core 2 processor, beginning a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel expands its sustainability commitments through ambitious environmental and social impact goals.

Sustainability Questions

Technology requires energy, materials, water, and manufacturing. Improving how those resources are used can reduce environmental impact while allowing technology to continue advancing.

Companies can improve energy efficiency, use renewable energy, reduce waste, conserve water, and design products that require fewer resources.

Responsible innovation means considering both what technology can accomplish and how creating that technology affects people, communities, and the environment.
